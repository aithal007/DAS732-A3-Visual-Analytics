## The workflows

One main question (*can infrastructure and history explain and forecast which countries get online, and does feeding
each iteration's inferences back into the data change the answer?*) is answered by three unrolled Keim workflows, one
per family of methods and one per team member. Each workflow diagram carries its question and hypotheses with
verdicts, the figures plugged into the stage that produced them, a numbered analysis path, and one edge labelled
"Feedback loop" whose transformation changes the data (not just a filter).

| Workflow | Owner | Iteration 1 (raw) | Feedback loop T | Iteration 2 (transformed) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| W1 Statistics | Parikshith | V1, V1d, V2, V3 | artefact rules; per-owner mobile; each curve in its own time `t - t0` and scale `y/L`; headroom | V9, V1c, V1e, V1f |
| W2 Unsupervised | Sindhoor | V4, V5, V5b, V6, V6b, V6c | snapshot to 2000-2025 trajectories; Euclidean to DTW | V8a, V8, V8d |
| W3 Supervised | Varshith | V7 | 13 history features from W1's inferences; in-fold MICE; drift-triggered conformal re-calibration | V10, V11, V12b, V13, V12 |

V0 shows the three workflows together and V14 is the synthesis.

## Viva preparation: likely questions

- **Where is the feedback loop?** Each workflow has one red edge labelled "Feedback loop" (V0, W1-W3). Table III of the report lists every inference, its evidence, the transformation it triggers and why the transformation is more than a filter.
- **Why "unrolled"?** A cyclic diagram cannot show which pass produced which inference. Unrolling gives two iterations with no self-loops and one labelled loop edge per workflow.
- **What makes W1's transformation significant?** Every curve is re-expressed in its own time (years since take-off) and scale (share of saturation), and the gap as headroom: a new coordinate system. In it the curves collapse (spread 32 to 10 pp, V1c).
- **What makes W2's transformation significant?** The unit of analysis changes from a 2024 snapshot to a 26-year curve, and the distance from Euclidean to DTW. 37 of 133 countries change group (V8d).
- **How do you know Fiji is an error and Monaco is not?** Persistence (V6b): Monaco is flagged every year, Fiji only in 2023-24, with flat ownership (V3).
- **Why group the CV folds by country?** Rows of the same country are near-duplicates across years. Random K-fold would leak a country's own history into its test fold. Grouping tests generalisation to an unseen country.
- **Is any feature leaking the target?** No. A guard rejects duplicates, counts and any column named "internet". Features are past-only (the landline peak is an expanding maximum). The trajectory clusters use the target and are deliberately not used as a feature.
- **Why k = 3?** k = 2 has the highest silhouette but misassigns two countries and only separates low from high. Within k = 3 to 6, k = 3 has the best silhouette. It is only moderately stable (bootstrap ARI 0.64; 15 border countries; V5b), and the report says so.
- **Is the leapfrog test (V1f) causal?** No. The landline peak also stands for the national investment and income that built both networks, and no income variable is used by design. V1f shows only that mobile saturation without landlines did not come with internet use (median 44% vs 93% online, p = 1e-11; rho 0.86 within Africa).
- **Why DTW, and why is its eta-squared lower?** Countries follow the same S-curve shifted in time (V1, V1c). DTW aligns shifts within a 5-year band (V8a), so it groups by shape; timing alone is separated better by Euclidean k-means (eta-squared 0.82 vs 0.59).
- **What is PSI?** PSI = Σ (cur − base)·ln(cur/base) over the baseline's deciles. Thresholds: below 0.1 keep the model; 0.1 to 0.2 evaluate; above 0.2 retrain or re-calibrate.
- **What is CQR?** Fit the quantile models on years up to 2017 and score 2018-20, using the score max(lo − y, y − hi). Take the ⌈(n+1)(1−α)⌉/n quantile of the scores and widen the 2021-25 band by it.
- **Why does phone ownership top SHAP?** Ownership is observed in only 11% of rows. Elsewhere it is a MICE estimate built from the other indicators, so it acts as a composite index. This is a stated limitation.
- **Causation?** No. There is no income variable, by design (the question is about infrastructure), so all results are associations.
